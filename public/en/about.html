About W.Y. Group

Connecting China, Europe and global ports through an integrated maritime platform

W.Y. Group is an international maritime industry group headquartered in Germany, with a strategic focus on ship full-lifecycle services and the FEX digital platform. The founder brings over 30 years of experience in large state-owned enterprise management, the maritime industry chain, and bulk commodity trade. Drawing on deep expertise from Hong Kong, Singapore, and Germany, the Group promotes cross-border resource synergy and cross-sector industrial integration.

W.Y. (Europe) Holding GmbH, based in Hamburg, is the Group Headquarters, with subsidiaries and operating companies including: FEX Global Holdings Limited and FEX Marine Service Co., Limited (Hong Kong), Eureka (Germany) GmbH, FEX Shipping & Supply Co., Ltd. (Qingdao, China), W.Y. (Singapore) Holding Pte Ltd, W.Y. (Singapore) Pte. Ltd., and W.Y. Development d.o.o. (Croatia).

Group Positioning

How the Group Operates

Resource Integration

Chinese supply chain, European resources and a global port network, coordinated from the Hamburg headquarters.

Project Delivery

Newbuilding, sale & purchase, finance & leasing, repair, offshore services, supply chain, logistics and port services, delivered by operating companies worldwide.

Platform Operation

The FEX digital platform accumulates data, standardizes services and scales the Group's offering globally from its Hong Kong platform headquarters.

Group Structure

German Headquarters + Hong Kong Platform Headquarters + Operating Companies

W.Y. Group's structure is built around integrated resources, coordinated execution and global delivery:

  • W.Y. (Europe) Holding GmbH — Hamburg, Germany. Group headquarters: strategic planning, global resource coordination and the European client interface.
  • FEX Global Holdings Limited — Hong Kong. Owns and operates the FEX digital platform and global port-node network.
  • FEX Marine Service Co., Limited — Hong Kong. Offshore and marine service operating company.
  • Eureka (Germany) GmbH — Germany operating company for European client development, chartering, S&P, finance and port services.
  • FEX Shipping & Supply Co., Ltd. — Qingdao, China. China supply chain, shipyards and port services.
  • W.Y. (Singapore) Holding Pte Ltd & W.Y. (Singapore) Pte. Ltd. — Singapore regional hub and Southeast Asia maritime trade.
  • W.Y. Development d.o.o. — Croatia. EU landing point, industrial park and overseas warehousing.
