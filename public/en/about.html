About W.Y. Group

Connecting China, Europe and global ports through an integrated maritime platform

W.Y. Group is an international maritime industry group headquartered in Hamburg, Germany, with a strategic focus on ship full-lifecycle services and the FEX digital platform. The founder brings over 30 years of experience in large state-owned enterprise management, the maritime industry chain, and bulk commodity trade, and the Group draws on deep expertise from Hong Kong, Singapore, and Germany to promote cross-border resource synergy and cross-sector industrial integration.

Group Positioning

How the Group Operates

Resource Integration

Chinese supply chain, European resources and a global port network, coordinated from the Hamburg headquarters.

Project Delivery

Newbuilding, sale & purchase, finance & leasing, repair, offshore services, supply chain, logistics and port services, delivered by operating companies worldwide.

Platform Operation

The FEX digital platform accumulates data, standardizes services and scales the Group's offering globally from its Hong Kong platform headquarters.

Group Structure
