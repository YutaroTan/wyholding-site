One Group, One Platform

Operating Companies of W.Y. Group

W.Y. (Europe) Holding GmbH coordinates the Group's strategy from Hamburg. Day-to-day services — ship repair and supply, digital trade, yacht building and industrial development — are delivered by dedicated operating companies, each with its own site. Explore them below.

Eureka (Germany) GmbH

The Group's European business execution platform, based in Hamburg. Connects European customers, China's maritime industry and the global port network — covering ship repair, inspection and port supply, product representation and sales, and cross-border e-commerce.

Visit eurekaeu.com

FEX Global Platform

Headquartered in Hong Kong under FEX Global Holdings Limited, FEX is the Group's unified brand for globalization, platformization and digitalization — a port-based e-commerce, ship supply and logistics platform. The site is currently in development.

Visit fex.global

FEX Yacht

The Group's yacht and leisure boat brand. Through FEX Yacht, the Group designs, builds and trades aluminum yachts, sport fishing boats, coastal leisure boats and commercial catamarans for the global market.

Visit fexyacht.com

Croatia Industrial Park

W.Y. Development d.o.o. leads the Group's EU landing point — an industrial park, airport cargo centre, overseas warehousing and supply chain finance hub connecting Central and Eastern Europe to the wider network.
