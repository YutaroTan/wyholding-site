About W.Y. Group

An international maritime industry group, coordinated from Germany

W.Y. (Europe) Holding GmbH, based in Hamburg, is the German headquarters of W.Y. Group — an international maritime industry group with a strategic focus on ship full-lifecycle services and the FEX digital platform. From Hamburg, the Group coordinates strategic planning, global resource integration and its European client interface.

Operationally, the Group works through FEX Global Holdings Limited (Hong Kong), which owns and operates the FEX platform, and a network of operating companies across Germany, China, Singapore and Croatia — including Eureka (Germany) GmbH, the Group's European business execution platform.
